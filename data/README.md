## Roman Urdu Phishing SMS Dataset (Zenodo)

- **Name:** Roman Urdu Phishing SMS Dataset, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.21810885](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21810885), author: Siddiq.
- **Contents:** 1,000 messages: 595 legitimate and 405 phishing, across 16 source types. After the template-leakage fix, these messages group into 189 templates. This is a highly templated dataset and should not be treated as representative real-world performance.
- **How obtained:** Download `phishing_dataset_1000.xlsx` from the Zenodo record into `data/raw/`, then run `python src/text/prepare_zenodo.py` from the repository root. This creates `data/processed/roman_urdu_zenodo.csv`, including raw/normalized fingerprint columns and the merged template IDs.
- **License:** Not verified. Do not redistribute the workbook or processed dataset until the license is confirmed on the Zenodo page.
- **Citation:** Siddiq. Roman Urdu Phishing SMS Dataset. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21810885
- **Committed to Git:** Neither the source workbook nor `data/processed/roman_urdu_zenodo.csv` is committed; both are git-ignored. The processed file contains dataset messages and must not be redistributed until its license is confirmed.

## Synthetic Roman Urdu starter data

- **Name:** `data/processed/roman_urdu.csv`.
- **How obtained:** Generated by `src/text/build_roman_urdu.py` from hand-authored message templates and placeholder values. It contains 60 synthetic template-generated messages and is starter data only, not a representative evaluation corpus.
- **License and citation:** Project-generated examples; no separate external dataset citation or formal license is specified.
- **Committed to Git:** Yes.

## Hand-written Roman Urdu starter data

- **Name:** `data/processed/roman_urdu_manual.csv`.
- **How obtained:** Hand-written starter examples.
- **Provenance and privacy:** Written by the project author with AI assistance. Mock OTP and tracking values are used; the examples contain no real personal data.
- **License and citation:** Project-authored examples; no separate external citation or formal license is specified.
- **Committed to Git:** Yes.

## Audio datasets used in Google Colab

These datasets were used for experiments in Google Colab only and are not stored in this repository.

### In-The-Wild

- **Name:** In-The-Wild audio deepfake dataset.
- **How obtained:** Obtain it from the dataset authors' project resources; the audio used in the experiments was handled in Google Colab and is not included here.
- **License:** CC BY-SA 4.0, as stated for the dataset.
- **Citation:** Mueller et al. “Does Audio Deepfake Detection Generalize?” arXiv:2203.16263.
- **Committed to Git:** No.

## Roman Urdu Phishing SMS Dataset (processed copy)
Source: "Roman Urdu Phishing SMS Dataset for Smishing Detection Research", Ghufran Siddiq, University of Management and Technology, Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21810885. License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
Changes made: cleaned and deduplicated; added columns source_type, template_id, text_norm, k_raw and k_norm (template ids come from union-find over masked message fingerprints). The scam and legitimate labels are the original labels.
Any reuse must credit the original authors and indicate that changes were made.
